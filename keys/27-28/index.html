The card that chose you

I give freely and with joy.

This is Key 27, the Gift of Altruism. Real giving comes from fullness, not obligation. When you give because you want to, it nourishes you as much as the person receiving.

Turn the card around

I embrace all of who I am, light and shadow.

Every key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 28, the Gift of Totality, a reminder to include yourself in your care.

Try this

Cup your hands together in front of you, as if holding something precious. Imagine filling them first for yourself, then offering what overflows to someone you love.

Sit with it
