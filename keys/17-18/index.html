The card that chose you

I see the bigger picture with my heart.

This is Key 17, the Gift of Far-sightedness. The mind sees details, while the heart sees the whole story. Looking with your heart helps the small worries fall into place.

Turn the card around

I grow with kindness towards myself.

Every Gene Key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 18, the Gift of Integrity, which brings that same kindness to how you see yourself.

Try this

Bring a worry to mind. Now imagine looking back at it a year from now, from somewhere peaceful. Place a hand on your heart and take a slow breath. What feels different from there?

Sit with it

What does your heart see that your mind was missing?

The card that chose you

I grow with kindness towards myself.

This is Key 18, the Gift of Integrity. Wanting to improve is healthy. Criticising yourself along the way isn't needed. Growth flourishes in kindness.

Try this

Sit with it
