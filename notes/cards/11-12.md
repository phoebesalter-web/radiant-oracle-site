# Card 11 · 12

Status: colour pilot live on the website, waiting for Phoebe and Lee's feedback.

## Key 11, Guiding Dreams
- Affirmation: I honour the dreams that light me up.
- Colour: twilight lavender #c9b6e4 (deep tone for text #5e4a86)
- Feeling: dreamy, visionary, the light you steer by.

## Key 12, Brave Words
- Affirmation: I choose my words with care and courage.
- Colour: clear throat blue #a9d2e6 (deep tone for text #2f6683)
- Feeling: clear, honest, a voice finding its shape.

## Art brief for the card image
- Lavender at the Key 11 end, flowing into clear blue at the Key 12 end.
- Imagery idea: a soft dream-light or star at the 11 end; a gentle open line or ripple, like a voice or breath, at the 12 end; the two meeting in the middle.
- Key 11 text upright at the top: "Key 11 · Guiding Dreams" and "I honour the dreams that light me up."
- Key 12 text upside down at the bottom: "Key 12 · Brave Words" and "I choose my words with care and courage."
- QR code to radiantoracle.com/keys/11-12/ with the line "Every card holds a pair. Discover how they meet."
