The card that chose you

I have the courage to feel what I feel.

This is Key 25, the Gift of Acceptance. Feelings aren't right or wrong. Letting yourself feel them, fully and kindly, takes courage, and it's how they move through.

Turn the card around

I delight in being alive in my body.

Every key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 46, the Gift of Delight: what opens up when you stop pushing feelings away, the simple pleasure of being alive.

Try this

Place one hand on your chest and one on your belly. Breathe slowly and say silently, "Whatever I'm feeling is welcome." Let it be there, just as it is.

Sit with it

What feeling have you been holding at arm's length?
What happens when you let it in?

The card that chose you

I delight in being alive in my body.

This is Key 46, the Gift of Delight. Your body isn't just something that carries you around. It's how you taste, touch, move and feel the joy of being here.

Turn the card around

I have the courage to feel what I feel.

Every key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 25, the Gift of Acceptance, which lets every part of you be welcome, so delight can flow freely.

Try this

Stretch slowly in whatever way your body wants to. Notice one sensation that feels good: warmth, softness, a loosening. Linger there for a breath or two.

Sit with it

What does your body love?
