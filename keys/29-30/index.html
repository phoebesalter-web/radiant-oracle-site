The card that chose you

I commit to myself, again and again.

This is Key 29, the Gift of Commitment. Commitment isn't a single promise. It's saying yes to yourself again each day, especially on the days it's hard.

Turn the card around

I hold my desires lightly.

Every key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 30, the Gift of Lightness, which keeps your commitment joyful rather than heavy.

Try this

Place both hands on your belly and feel it rise and fall. With each breath out, say silently, "Yes." Let each yes be to you.

Sit with it

What would it mean to choose yourself today?

The card that chose you

I hold my desires lightly.

This is Key 30, the Gift of Lightness. Wanting things is natural. Holding them lightly means enjoying the longing without needing everything to happen right now.

Turn the card around

I commit to myself, again and again.

Every key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 29, the Gift of Commitment, which keeps you steady while your desires unfold.

Try this

Hold out an open palm and imagine a feather resting on it. Feel how lightly you'd hold it so it doesn't blow away or get crushed. Hold your wishes like that.

Sit with it

What are you longing for?
How would it feel to hold that longing more lightly?
