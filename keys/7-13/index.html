The card that chose you

I lead simply by being myself.

This is Key 7, Quiet Leadership. You don't need a title to lead. When you live true to yourself, others quietly find their way by your example.

Turn the card around

I listen deeply, to others and to me.

Every key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 13, Deep Listening: the listening that makes your guidance wise.

Try this

Stand or sit tall, chin level, shoulders relaxed. Take a breath and feel what it's like to simply take up your own space.

Sit with it
