The card that chose you

I honour the dreams that light me up.

This is Key 11, the Gift of Idealism. Somewhere in you is a picture of how life could be. It isn't a fantasy to grow out of. It's a light to steer by.

Turn the card around

I choose my words with care and courage.

Every Gene Key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 12, the Gift of Discrimination. A dream stays a dream until it's spoken. Key 12 is the voice that gives your vision shape: what to say, when, and to whom.

Try this

Close your eyes and picture a dream you hold, in as much colour and detail as you can. Notice where in your body you feel it light up.

Sit with it

Which dream came to mind first?
Who would you need to say it to, and what would you say?
