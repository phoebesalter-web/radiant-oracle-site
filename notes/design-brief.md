# Design brief: Phoebe Affirmations

## The deck

- 32 poker-size cards printed **landscape** (88 mm wide x 63 mm tall), plus one guide card.
- Each card holds two partnered keys, one affirmation in each half. The second affirmation is printed upside down (rotated 180 degrees), so whichever way the card lands decides which affirmation chose the person.
- On phones the website shows the cards portrait, because that's how phone screens work. The printed cards stay landscape.
- One shared card back, landscape: the Phoebe star design, "Phoebe · Affirmations · Let the card choose you."
- Each card face carries a QR code to its page, for example radiantoracle.com/keys/11-12/ (currently phoebesalter-web.github.io/radiant-oracle-site/keys/11-12/).
- Line near the QR code: "Every card holds a pair. Discover how they meet."

## Purpose and voice

- A deck for finding your way to your yes. Affirmation cards for gentle empowerment.
- Your answers are already within you. Allow each card to show you how your own knowing speaks.
- Affirmations are positively stated, present tense, first person. Questions are in the first person too, as the reader's own inner voice.
- Keys use Phoebe's own names (for example Key 11, Guiding Dreams), not Gene Keys terminology.

## Overall look

- Cream and warm paper ground, soft gold line work and stars, gentle pastel washes.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond italic for affirmations and headings; Lato for body text.
- Gold for text is the deeper, bolder gold (#87621f) so it stays readable.

## Colour per card

Each card has two colours, one per key, chosen intuitively by the feeling of each key (colour therapy, not Human Design centres). The two colours blend into each other, from one half of the card to the other, like the two affirmations meeting. On the web page, the colours wash from the top-left corner to the bottom-right.

Keep: enough contrast that every word is easy to read on every colour, and a family feel across all 32 cards.

## Card art brief, general

For each card image:
- Landscape, 88 x 63 mm, with 3 mm bleed on all sides; 300 dpi.
- One affirmation upright in the top half, its partner upside down in the bottom half, each with its key number and name.
- The pair's two colours blending from one half into the other, with soft gold detailing.
- Leave clear space for a QR code of at least 18 mm, with high contrast behind it.
- Keep all text at least 4 mm inside the trim.
