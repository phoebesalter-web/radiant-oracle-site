The card that chose you

I trust what has already begun in me.

This is Key 53, What's Already Begun. Something in you is already moving. Before the mind decides, the body often knows where it's being drawn. Trusting that pull, rather than hesitating, lets new beginnings unfold naturally.

I rise, and I lift others with me.

Every key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 54, Rising Together: the upward energy that grows from what's already begun.
