The card that chose you

I speak from my heart's true intention.

This is Key 26, Heartfelt Words. When your words come from your heart, people feel it. You don't need to persuade or impress, only to mean what you say.

Turn the card around

Together, we are more.

Every key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 45, Shared Purpose: what heartfelt words create, real connection and shared purpose.

Try this

Place a hand on your heart. Think of something you want to say to someone. Before you speak, ask your heart, "What do I really mean?" Notice the answer.

Sit with it
