This is Key 57, Quiet Knowing. Your intuition speaks softly, through a hunch, a feeling or a gentle nudge. The more you listen, the clearer it becomes.

I have the courage to be who I am.

Every key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 51, Bold Steps, which gives you the courage to act on what you know.

Try this

Think of a choice in front of you. Turn it into a simple yes-or-no question, such as "Is this right for me?" or "Do I want to go?" Close your eyes, ask it silently, and notice the very first feeling in your body: an opening and lightness, or a tightening and heaviness. That first feeling is your knowing.
