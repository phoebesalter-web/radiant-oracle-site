The card that chose you

I seek to understand before I answer.

This is Key 4, the Gift of Understanding. The mind loves quick answers, but real understanding takes a breath first. It listens for the whole picture.

Turn the card around

I am free to let old patterns go.

Every Gene Key has a partner, its other half. Yours is Key 49, the Gift of Revolution: what understanding makes possible, the freedom to change what no longer serves you.

Try this

Before answering anything today, try one slow breath first. Practise now: think of a question on your mind, breathe in and out slowly, and notice what arrives in the pause.
